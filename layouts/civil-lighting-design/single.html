Lighting design has always meant architectural lighting design. Buildings, facades, interiors. That makes sense: most lighting lives in those spaces. But some spaces cannot be divided into managed horizontal and vertical planes, because nobody controls them. The road geometry is given. The trees, the traffic, and the weather do what they do. This newsletter is for the people lighting those places, whether you work there already, are looking for tools and concepts you can apply today, or are an architectural lighting designer curious about what changes when the calculation planes are gone.
